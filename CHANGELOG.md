# Changelog

All notable changes to Phlant. Dates are the deploy dates.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-10-01
- UI/UX overhaul: sidebar + bottom navigation, global date bar with Moon pill, light/dark/auto theme, first-run setup, Today page led by a one-line verdict, tabbed Garden panel.
- Site page: Leaflet/OpenStreetMap map picker, coordinate paste (decimal or DMS), USDA SSURGO soil map for 0.25–200 acres with per-unit shares.
- Wind climatology: three years of hourly ERA5 → wind rose and month × direction heatmap; the garden plan follows the month's dominant wind.
- Garden: shadow casters (house, tree, fence, shed, greenhouse, wall, path), plan orientation, hourly shadow footprints, sun hours per bed, templates, undo, import/export, and a three.js 3D view lit by the real Sun.
- Sky: five-clock explainer, ecliptic ring with planets, exact sign ingress times from IAU boundaries, 20-year pattern grid.
- GitHub Pages deployment at phlant.robbiemed.org.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-01
- All seven tabs; offline site packs (soil, water gauges, frost statistics, elevation); weather strip.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-01
- Ephemeris core, seven tradition engines with consensus, plant database, Today and Calendar views.
